    LEARN THE SPACE

    Go beyond the board.

    Your first turn

    Drag the cube to rotate it. Select a slice with the slider, or switch to 2D for a flat view. Tap an intersection to draft a stone. Add connected stones if Omino is on, then press Place turn. You can also use X/Y/Z coordinates (1-based).

    Translucent placement previews

    Drag a piece from the Drag stone handle onto the cube, or tap an empty point. It remains translucent until confirmed. Tap a preview stone to commit the whole turn, or use Place turn. Drag a preview stone to pick it back up and try another point. An invalid drop leaves its preceding preview intact. This works in All XYZ, 3D slice, and 2D layer views. Turn it off in View to use selection and the Place turn button without drag or tap confirmation. In Omino, tapping any preview confirms the entire drafted shape.

    Connections & captures

    With diagonals on, stones connect through all 26 neighboring points; otherwise they use the six face-adjacent points. Empty adjacent points are liberties. Surround an opposing group to remove its last liberty and capture it. Suicide is illegal. Recreating an earlier board and turn state is illegal; passing remains allowed.

    Omino & Borderless

    Omino allows up to four uses per turn; Black's first turn is limited to two. Regular and Tier Stones drafted that turn must connect to each other. Ghosts and neutral pieces cost uses but do not connect the shape. Each placement resolves captures in draft order, and the entire turn must be legal. No komi with Omino. Borderless connects all three pairs of opposite cube faces, including diagonal wraps.

    Ghost Stones

    A ghost costs one use and grants friendly physical groups a liberty, without connecting them. Either side can replace it with a physical or neutral piece for the normal placement cost. Removing a ghost is not a capture. Ghosts score no points; for territory traversal their points are open.

    Obstacles & coins

    Red obstacles and silver coins are neutral, permanent, uncapturable blockages. They cost one use, block liberties, and score nothing. Coins can also be seeded before play. Neither kind claims territory on its own. Piece supplies are unlimited.

    Tier Stones

    Tier 1, 2, and 3 cost 2, 3, and 4 uses. A higher tier supports a lower friendly group's liberties; lower stones do not support higher groups. Stones of the same tier may connect through higher tiers. A zero-liberty tier group is captured only if an adjacent attacking stone of the mover has equal or higher tier. A protected opposing group can remain at zero liberties; deliberately leaving your own group without liberties is still suicide.

    Stoical & First Capture

    Stoical forbids captures in the turn immediately after an opposing capture. We retain repetition prevention and allow custom scoring combinations. In FFA, this applies to the immediately preceding player; in Rengo it applies only across opposing teams. First Capture ends immediately on the first captured physical stone. The mover earns capture credit.

    Score targets

    Optionally win at 25, 50, 75, or 100 points. After your completed turn, including a pass, reaching or exceeding the target with the selected scoring method wins immediately. Rengo uses the team total; komi applies when enabled. Targets are off by default. Handicap setup and individual draft placements do not trigger a win; the complete legal Omino turn is scored. First Capture takes priority if both conditions happen together. Counting and Territory count current controlled regions without dead-group review, so an open cube can reach the target on the first move. Use Stones Only or Capture Only for a target based on stones or prisoners.

    Ending & scoring

    When every seat passes consecutively, scoring begins. Tap physical groups to mark them dead, then accept the score or resume. Empty regions bordered exclusively by one side's physical stones belong to that side; mixed or ownerless regions are neutral. Wrapping and diagonals apply. Counting scores stones plus territory; Territory scores territory plus prisoners. Capture Only and Stones Only are also available. Dead prisoners in FFA are credited only when exactly one opposing side borders them. Early territory estimates can be very large in an open 3D space.

    Teams, clocks & saves

    Rengo seats share a color and clock. Choose interleaved or team-block order. One completed Omino turn earns one increment. Overtime uses resettable periods. Local clocks pause when the page is hidden or you choose Pause. Undo is unrestricted locally; in AI play it returns to your preceding turn. Online multiplayer is deferred.

    The three checkpoints track completed turns, not individual Omino placements. Before ten/twenty turns exist those slots are empty. There is one manual browser slot and ten numbered device-file slots. Save files use this game's versioned JSON format, preserving variants and repetition state. Clearing browser data removes browser saves; export backups to keep them.
